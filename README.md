One sentence, 201 characters, pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance:

> Meet the Tube Tribunal: a toothpaste tube that squeezes itself from the bottom and leaves a one-star review of whoever squeezed the middle on the bathroom mirror, ending the bathroom’s oldest cold war.

The annoyance is tiny and universal — someone always squeezes the toothpaste tube from the middle. The invention is absurd but weirdly plausible: a tube that squeezes itself from the bottom, correctly, every time, and then passes judgement, leaving a one-star review of the offender on the bathroom mirror. The laugh comes from the invention itself: the tube is both housekeeper and critic.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, where the sealed scorer checks it is UTF-8 text of 1 to 220 characters, and the house judge compares it two at a time on how funny the invention is, how clever the fix is and how original the idea is.
